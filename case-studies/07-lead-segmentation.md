# Lead Segmentation & Enrichment

**Role:** analyst · **When:** 2026 · **Stack:** Salesforce exports, Excel

## The problem
The prospect database had free-text job titles and many blank region and industry fields, so segments for email and paid campaigns were rough.

## What I did
- Clustered free-text job titles into persona groups (for example FP&A and finance leaders, BI and analytics, data engineering, IT) for targeting.
- Profiled which records were missing region, job title or industry.
- Wrote an enrichment spec: which fields to fill, from which sources, the match rules, and how enriched data should flow back into the CRM.
