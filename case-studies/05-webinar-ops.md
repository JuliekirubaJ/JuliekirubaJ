# Webinar Operations Pipeline

**Role:** owner · **When:** 2025–2026 · **Stack:** Zoom Webinars exports, Pardot, PowerShell, Excel, AI agents

## Context
3–4 webinars a month. Each one needs a registration page, an email sequence (invite → 1-day-to-go → day-of → follow-up), LinkedIn and community promotion, a run-of-show, a replay, short clips and a report.

## What I built
- **Standard timeline and task set:** adding a webinar generates its full task list with owners and dates in the team tracker.
- **Data consolidation:** a script and agent merge Zoom attendee, poll and Q&A exports into the master webinar report while keeping its formulas and layout, so registrations, attendance and engagement can be compared across webinars.
- **Post-event package by T+3:** a companion web page (summary, takeaways, FAQ, resources) drafted from the transcript, a cut-list of 5–10 short clips with timestamps, and three video deliverables tracked per webinar.

## Outcome
- Post-webinar reporting no longer depends on manual copy-paste.
- Every webinar gets a replay page and clips, so it keeps working after the live date.
